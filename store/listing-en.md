## Full description

Build better habits with a little friend by your side.

Pawtiva turns small daily habits into a cozy routine. Choose a kitten or a puppy as your companion, tick off what you did today, and watch your friend grow with you.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create simple daily habits with an icon, a color and an optional note.
• Mark a habit as done and earn 10 XP and 5 PawCoins.
• Level up together: every 100 XP is a new level, with a small celebration.
• Forgot to check something off? You can update the last 7 days.
• A missed day never resets your progress. Your pet is never punished.

YOUR COMPANION
• Pick a kitten or a puppy and give it a name.
• Your pet reacts to your progress: happy, excited, sleepy at night, and celebrating when you finish the day.
• Switch pets any time in Settings. Your habits, XP, level, statistics and accessories always stay.

COSMETIC SHOP
• Spend PawCoins on accessories: a cap, round glasses, headphones, a winter scarf, a flower and a royal crown.
• Preview items on your pet before buying, then wear them or take them off whenever you like.
• PawCoins are earned only by completing habits. They cannot be bought with real money.

STATISTICS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
• See total completions, XP, level and PawCoins earned.
• Daily completion rate, weekly and monthly progress, a calendar view and recent history.
• Unlock achievements such as First Step, Perfect Day and a 7-day streak.

GENTLE REMINDERS
• One optional daily reminder at the time you choose.
• Pawtiva asks for notification permission only if you turn reminders on.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline. No account, no sign-in.
• No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
• No analytics or tracking. Your data stays on your device.
• Export your data or reset the app from Settings.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Turkish and English.
• Light and dark theme, or follow your device.
• Screen reader labels, large text support and reduced-motion options.

Small steps add up. Start today, together with your little friend.
