# Pawtiva Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Pawtiva ("the app") is a habit tracker with a virtual pet companion, developed by Nisa Efendioğlu ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

**In short: Pawtiva works entirely on your device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your personal data.**

## 1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following information **only on your device**:

- Your habits (name, optional description, icon, color) and the days you completed them.
- Your progress: XP, level, PawCoins balance and transactions, purchased and equipped pet accessories, and unlocked achievements.
- Your preferences: chosen pet (kitten or puppy), pet name, language, theme, date format, reminder setting and time, animation preference, and whether you finished the welcome screens.

This data is stored in the app's private storage (a local database and a local preferences file). Other apps cannot read it. It is never sent to us.

## 2. Information we collect

None. The app:

- has no user accounts, sign-in or registration;
- has no servers and does not connect to the internet to send or receive your data;
- contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs that collect data;
- does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## 3. Permissions

- **Notifications (optional).** If you turn on daily reminders, Android asks you to allow notifications (Android 13 and newer). The permission is used only to show your reminder on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app or revoke the permission in Android settings at any time.
- **Run at startup / alarms.** The app reschedules your reminder after the device restarts or the time zone changes. No data leaves your device.

## 4. Android backup and device transfer

The app allows Android's built-in backup. If you have turned on backup on your device, Android may include the app's database and preferences in **your own Google account backup**, and copy them when you move to a new device. This backup is created and managed by Android and Google under your account and their privacy terms. The developer cannot see or access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

## 5. Your choices and control

- **Export:** Settings → Data and privacy → Export my data creates a JSON file with all your app data, saved to a location you choose.
- **Reset:** Settings → Data and privacy → Reset app data permanently deletes all habits, progress, purchases, achievements and preferences from your device after you confirm.
- **Uninstall:** Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your device (except any copy in your own Android backup).

## 6. Virtual currency

PawCoins are a virtual reward earned only by completing habits. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought with real money and cannot be exchanged or withdrawn. The app contains no payments.
